lifevault privacy policy

Effective October 20, 2026

What lifevault is

lifevault is a personal data tool built and used by one person, its owner. It is not offered to the public, and no one else has an account. The only person whose data is collected is the owner.

What is collected

With the owner's own consent, given each time the owner links an account through Plaid Link, lifevault receives account balances, transactions, investment holdings and loan details from the owner's financial institutions. It also stores the owner's own health and activity data.

How it is used

The data is used only by the owner, to view and analyse the owner's own finances and health. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising, and no one else can see it.

How it is protected

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access tokens and secrets are kept in managed secret storage, not in code. Access is limited to the owner and protected by multi-factor authentication.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the owner uses the tool. When the owner disconnects an institution, access is revoked through Plaid and that institution's stored data is deleted within 30 days.

Your rights and contact

Because the owner is the only data subject, requests are handled directly by the owner. Questions about this policy can be raised through the project's page on GitHub: github.com/AllanSlette/lifevault-link.
